# Ролик H1-01 — Боль

Проект: Art Window. Обещание: A new painting on your phone every day — picked to fit your screen, never chopped to fit it.
Длина: 15 с. Формат: `references/clip-style.md` скилла `demand-test`.

## Хук (0–2 с)

Текст на экране: **Wallpaper apps zoom into paintings.**
Источник формулировки: `docs/research/market_research.md`, Reddit → Android, строка 1 («can't find any way to have my background picture not zoom in»)
