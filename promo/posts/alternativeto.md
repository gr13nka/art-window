## Short description

A new public-domain painting as your wallpaper every day, on your desktop, phone and TV.

## Description

Art Window hangs a different painting on your screen each day, by itself. There is
nothing to search, pick or download.

The paintings come from the open collections of The Met, the National Gallery of Art,
the Cleveland Museum of Art, SMK, the Rijksmuseum and the Getty: about 18,000 of them, all public
domain.

- On the desktop the painting is shown whole, fitted to the screen.
- On macOS it reaches every Space on every display.
- On Android it picks paintings shaped like your screen.
- On Android TV and Google TV it runs as a screensaver and a full-screen art mode.
- Keep the ones you love as favourites, and open any painting's museum page.

No account, no ads, no analytics. The daily painting costs nothing; a paid licence for
extra filters and styles is planned.
