# Remaining Play Console steps, in order

1. **Developer account.** Confirm the account type. A personal account created after November 2023 must run a closed test (step 8) before it may apply for production.
2. **Create the app.** Name "Art Window", default language English (United Kingdom), app (not game), free. Package name is `dev.artwindow` and cannot change later.
3. **Play App Signing.** In the first release, enrol in Play App Signing and choose to upload the existing key rather than let Google generate one. The `.aab` is signed by the release keystore from the `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_BASE64` secret, which then becomes the upload key. Keep that keystore: losing it needs a key reset through Play support. If you also want to keep sideloaded APK updates working, ask Play to export the app signing key rather than rely on a new one; otherwise the APK from GitHub and the Play build will be signed with different keys and cannot update each other.
4. **App content.** Complete each form: privacy policy URL, ads (none), app access (all functionality available without login), content rating questionnaire (no violence, sexual content or user-generated content; the paintings are museum art, and some include nudity, so answer that question honestly), target audience (13 and over is the safe pick; do not choose under-13 groups, which brings the Families policy), news app (no), data safety (see `data-safety.md`), government app (no), financial features (none), health (none).
5. **Store listing.** Paste from `listing.md`. Upload screenshots and graphics from `screenshots.md`. Category: Art & Design. Contact email and website.
6. **Form factors.** In Setup, add Android TV. Upload TV screenshots and the 1280 x 720 banner. The manifest already declares `leanback` and `touchscreen` as not required, so one bundle serves both. Review Play's TV app quality requirements before submitting (D-pad navigation, launcher banner, leanback launcher activity).
7. **Target API level.** The app targets 35. Check the level Play currently requires for new apps and updates before uploading; if it is higher, raise `targetSdk` and `compileSdk` in `app/build.gradle.kts` and retest.
8. **Closed test.** Create a closed testing track, upload the `.aab`, and recruit at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 days in a row. Keep a list of testers' Google accounts or a Google Group. Testers need Play install links, not APKs.
9. **Apply for production access.** After 14 days, answer Play's questions about the test and apply. Review can take a few days.
10. **Production release.** Create the release with the `.aab` from the GitHub Release (`art-window-android.aab`). The version code derived from `Cargo.toml` must be higher than any already uploaded.
11. **Each later release.** Bump `Cargo.toml`, tag, then upload the new `.aab` to Play.
