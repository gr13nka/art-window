Something new every day

Each day Art Window hangs a different painting from the open collections of six museums. You don’t search, pick or download anything. The day changes, and so does the picture.

Every Space, every display

macOS keeps a separate wallpaper for every Space on every display. Art Window reaches all of them, not just the one in front of you.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

Art Window is an independent project within the Alps project R&D hub, available at artwindow.alps-project.online. It is operated by Horis International Limited (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This policy covers this website, its sign-up form and the Art Window apps. The main Alps project site has its own Privacy Policy.

1What We Collect

When you ask for an install link or join the iPhone & iPad waitlist, we collect your email address, the platform you chose and the date you signed up. We collect nothing else.

2How We Use It

We use your email address only to send you the link to install Art Window on the platform you chose — or, for iPhone and iPad, to tell you when the app is out — and to send occasional updates about that release. Every email says how to unsubscribe. We never sell, rent, or share your address.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

3Where It Is Stored

Your email address is stored on a server we operate. We do not use any third-party email or storage service, and no one outside our team has access to it.

4How Long We Keep It

We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If the iPhone & iPad app does not launch, we delete the addresses that asked for it.

5The Apps

The Art Window apps for macOS, Windows, GNOME, Android and iOS need no account and contain no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. They send us nothing.

The only thing they do over the network is download the day’s painting directly from the museum that holds it, or from the image host that museum uses. Like any website you visit, that host sees your device’s IP address when it serves the picture. Your settings, favourites and the pictures themselves stay on your device.

On Android the app asks for internet access, permission to see whether the device is online, permission to set the wallpaper, and permission to start after the device restarts so the painting still changes. It asks for nothing else.

6Cookies and Analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. Our servers keep standard logs (such as IP addresses and browser types) solely for security and stable operation. The install link we send points to GitHub Releases, which has its own privacy policy.

7Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, or object to how we use it. Email us at the address below and we will respond within one month. You may also complain to the data-protection authority in your country.

8Children’s Privacy

The sign-up is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the address.

9Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If a change affects how we use your email address, we will notify you by email first.
