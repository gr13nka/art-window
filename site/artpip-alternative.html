Artpip is no longer available. Art Window does the same daily job on Windows and Mac.

About Artpip

Artpip was a Windows and Mac app that set a classic painting as the desktop wallpaper once a day. It was free, with an optional $10 Pro upgrade. It is discontinued and its website is offline.

What Art Window does differently

Art Window also changes the wallpaper once a day, with nothing to search, pick or download. The paintings come from The Met, the National Gallery of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art, SMK, the Rijksmuseum and the Getty, all in the public domain. By default each painting is shown whole, fitted to the screen over black. On a Mac it reaches every Space on every display. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Favourites keep the pictures you love.
