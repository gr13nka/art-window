A Muzei alternative

Muzei is a good app with a different idea. Art Window is for people who want the painting itself on the wallpaper.

About Muzei

Muzei is a free, open-source Android live wallpaper that refreshes your home screen daily with famous works of art. It is still maintained, with an update in September 2026. By design it blurs and dims the artwork behind your icons, and as a live wallpaper it zooms the picture to fill the screen.

What Art Window does differently

Art Window picks paintings shaped like your screen, so the day’s painting fills it with only a light trim. It sets a new one every day from The Met, the National Gallery of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art, SMK, the Rijksmuseum and the Getty, all in the public domain. There is no account, no ads and no analytics, and favourites keep the pictures you love. Art Window also runs on the desktop, and on Android TV and Google TV as a screensaver and an art mode.
