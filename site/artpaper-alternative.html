Looking for Artpaper on Windows, Android or GNOME? Art Window sets a new painting as your wallpaper every day on all three.

About Artpaper

Artpaper, by Gikken, offers 1,000+ hand-picked paintings as wallpapers. Gikken’s own site says “Artpaper is available for macOS, iOS and iPadOS at the moment” and invites people to leave an email for Android or Windows. The Mac version is free, and its current version needs macOS 26.

What Art Window does differently

Art Window runs on Windows 10 1809+ x64, Android 11+ and GNOME on Linux, and on macOS 11+. It sets a new public-domain painting every day with nothing to search, pick or download, from The Met, the National Gallery of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art, SMK, the Rijksmuseum and the Getty. On the desktop each painting is shown whole, fitted to the screen over black. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. iPhone & iPad are on a waitlist.
